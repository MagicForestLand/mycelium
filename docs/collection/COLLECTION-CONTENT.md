## From the mushroom to the forest

First the mushrooms. Then the forest.

A forest is never just its trees. It is roots, fungi, light, soil, and countless small lives making a place together. Mycelium begins with a gathering of little mushroom friends: curious faces, improbable hats, and hobbies worth making time for.

Mycelium is the branching network of fungal threads. Mushrooms are the fruiting bodies of some fungi. Many trees form partnerships with mycorrhizal fungi, exchanging sugars for help reaching water and mineral nutrients. Other fungi recycle organic matter. Our little friends celebrate these often unseen lives that help forests thrive.

We are raising funds for Magic Forest: to secure land in Spain, plant a forest, and create an Art Hub where people can make things, learn, and spend time together. Donations started the story. This collection is another way to help it grow.

Before public sales, our existing donors get the first invitation to claim a mushroom. It is our thank-you to the people who helped this idea take root. The donor mint is free. Network gas fees still apply. Claim eligibility and allowances are set for the donor phase.

Magic Forest began with people who believed in the idea and chose to help it take root. Mycelium Friends carries that same spirit: small characters, an on-chain collection, and a real-world project growing toward land, trees, and a place for creativity in Spain.

A small friend to keep. A real forest to help grow.
