A Mycelium Friend is a little on-chain mushroom with a face, a point of view, and something it likes doing. You choose all six parts, so the first thing you meet is a character that feels like yours.

Six parts. One particular friend.

Choose a stem, cap, temperament, glasses, accessory, and hobby. Tiny vector beads form the artwork, while its original traits, image, and descriptions live entirely on Ethereum. No reveal to wait for. No image server to keep alive.

Make it yours.

Choose from 32 stems, 128 caps, 32 temperaments, 16 glasses, 4 decorations and 32 hobby options. Glasses, decorations and hobbies are independent: choose any combination, or leave them off. The preview changes as you choose.

One combination. One ID.

268,435,456 possible combinations. Every friend has its own token ID, and each combination can be minted only once. No random mint. No reveal.

Made to live on-chain.

The SVG artwork and metadata are generated from the collection's stored vector traits. Every mushroom has a transparent canvas, with no external images needed to render it.

THE CONTRACT

One collection. One record.

Every myFriend is minted through the same ERC-721 collection contract. Its six birth traits are recorded on-chain, the original artwork and metadata are generated from on-chain data, and the same combination can never be minted twice.

THE PRICE SPIRAL

Every public mint moves one step.

#1FREE→ #20.0001→ #30.0002→ #40.0003→ #50.0004 ETH

The first public myFriend is free. Each public mint increases the next price by exactly 0.0001 ETH. Allowlist claims are free and do not advance the public curve.

Show contract proof
uint256 internal constant STEP = 100_000_000_000_000;

function price(uint256 minted, uint256 quantity)
    internal pure returns (uint256)
{
    return STEP * (quantity * minted
        + quantity * (quantity - 1) / 2);
}
ROYALTIES

5% is the ceiling.

Secondary-sale royalties use ERC-2981. The collection starts at 5%. The contract owner can lower the rate or restore it, but the permanent ceiling is 5%. Royalty payment still depends on marketplace support.

    if (bps > MAX_ROYALTY_BPS) revert InvalidRoyaltyBps(bps);

TWO MARKETS

A price is not found once. It keeps being found.

New myFriends enter through the Spiral builder. Existing myFriends meet new owners on OpenSeasecondary markets. One collection. Two markets watching each other.

PRIMARY · THE SPIRAL

Build on the Spiral

Every public mint raises the next price by 0.0001 ETH. The contract can describe 268,435,456 unique friends, 228. This is a mathematical horizon, not a sellout plan. We do not expect to reach it. The Spiral lets demand decide how far the collection grows, one visible step at a time.

SECONDARY · RESALE

Trade on OpenSeaTrade on secondary markets

Owners ask. Collectors answer. Existing friends find a price of their own. When resale wakes up, it can pull attention back to the Spiral. When the Spiral moves, it can make collectors look again at what already exists. Either market can push the other.

FOMO exists in every market. It can gather around a single 1/1 artwork. It can also gather around a 10k pfp collection that starts cheap and becomes expensive on the secondary market. The Spiral does not pretend to remove it. It makes the rule and the next price visible. Pause. Look at the art. Read the contract. Choose only the friend and price you genuinely want.

This is an experiment with a real purpose. We are trying to turn a collection into a forest and an Art Hub for a community. We want it to work, and we are searching in public for a new way to fund that shared dream. The Spiral can grow when people believe in it and find its natural edge when they do not. Wherever that edge appears, every step is visible and every myFriend brings the forest closer.

PRIVACY

Privacy, simply.

Magic Forest Friends LLC keeps only what is needed to run the mint safely, verify purchases and maintain accurate sales records. This is about our responsibilities as the seller—not your personal taxes.

What we keep

Before a mint, essential browser storage keeps your local choices and protected-mint recovery data on your device. When you press Mint, our server records the wallet address, chosen genotype, sale phase, a country-level network signal and the network address it observed, stored in encrypted form. If the mint proceeds, its transaction details are added after the blockchain confirms them. Ethereum records are public by design.

How it is used

We use this limited record to protect, recover and verify the purchase, prevent misuse and keep our own sales records accurate. The country signal does not decide who you are, where you live, whether you can mint or what you pay. It is supporting information only and can be wrong when a VPN or mobile network is involved.

Who can see it

The network address is encrypted and hidden from ordinary administration screens. Access is restricted to authorised record exports. Infrastructure providers may process the information only to operate the service. Nothing is automatically sent to tax authorities; information is disclosed only when the law requires it. We never sell it or use it for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

How long it stays—and your choices

An unconfirmed mint record is removed after 90 days. A confirmed sale record is kept for 10 years. You can ask to access or correct information, or exercise other applicable privacy rights, at hello@magicforest.land. Records that the law requires us to keep may remain for that period, and public Ethereum records cannot be changed by us.
